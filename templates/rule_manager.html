Intelligent Decision Support Rule Configuration

Map predicted probabilities into actionable retention interventions.

MCA Major Project
High Churn Risk Band
Probability > 60%

Triggers priority retention workflow for accounts at imminent risk of cancelling service.

Automated Recommendation:

Assign dedicated account manager, issue 20% contract renewal discount coupon, and fast-track open support tickets.

Medium Churn Risk Band
30% ≤ Probability ≤ 60%

Identifies customers showing early warning signs of disengagement or usage drop-off.

Automated Recommendation:

Trigger automated CSAT feedback survey, offer free feature training, and share product usage tips.

Low Churn Risk Band
Probability < 30%

Satisfied customers with high retention likelihood and strong account stability.

Automated Recommendation:

Standard engagement pattern. Target for cross-selling premium add-on subscriptions and referral rewards.
